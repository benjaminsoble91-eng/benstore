Benstore Versions

Install any past build to downgrade. Your apps, Apple IDs and parked apps stay on the phone.

Downgrade inside Benstore
  1. Benstore → Settings → turn on Sources.
  2. Browse tab → + → paste this link:
  3. Pick a version under Benstore Versions and install it.
Or download it here
  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone, then tap Get on the version you want.
  2. When the download finishes, open it (the download arrow in Safari, or Files → Downloads).
  3. Tap Share → Benstore. Benstore installs it over itself, like an update, and closes.
  4. To go back to the newest version, tap Update in Benstore's Settings like normal.
